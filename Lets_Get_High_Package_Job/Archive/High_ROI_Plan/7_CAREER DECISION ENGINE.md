# CAREER DECISION ENGINE

Version: 2.0

Purpose:

This document is the strategic decision engine of my Career Operating System.

Its responsibility is not to generate prompts.

Its responsibility is to determine the single highest-leverage action that will produce the greatest long-term improvement in my career.

Whenever multiple good options exist, this document provides the framework for choosing the best one.

It exists to eliminate decision fatigue.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Always identify the activity with the highest overall Career ROI.

Career ROI means the greatest long-term improvement relative to the time and effort invested.

Optimize for:

* Hiring Probability
* Practical Engineering Ability
* Portfolio Strength
* Interview Readiness
* Market Value
* Career Leverage
* Long-Term Salary Growth

Never optimize only for technical learning.

---

# MY LONG-TERM OBJECTIVE

My objective is to:

* Transition from my current DBA-related role into DevOps Engineering.
* Become confidently job-ready.
* Build production-quality engineering ability.
* Create convincing proof of skill.
* Increase long-term market value.
* Secure stronger engineering roles over time.

Every recommendation should support these objectives.

---

# MY CONSTRAINTS

Always assume:

* I work full-time.
* My available learning time is limited.
* My energy is limited.
* My attention is valuable.

If two paths produce similar career outcomes,

always recommend the simpler path.

Prefer leverage over completeness.

---

# CAREER ROI FRAMEWORK

Evaluate opportunities using these dimensions.

Technical Skill

Proof of Skill

Practical Confidence

Portfolio Quality

Interview Readiness

Recruiter Perception

Career Leverage

Long-Term Market Value

The strongest activities improve several dimensions simultaneously.

---

# DECISION FRAMEWORK

Whenever multiple options are available:

1. Remove the biggest blocker.

2. Strengthen practical engineering ability.

3. Produce visible proof of skill.

4. Improve interview readiness.

5. Increase recruiter confidence.

6. Improve long-term market value.

Learning new technology should only happen if it directly supports these goals.

---

# HIGH-LEVERAGE ACTIVITIES

Prefer activities such as:

* Building production-style projects
* Improving existing projects
* Troubleshooting real systems
* Writing better documentation
* Practicing architecture explanations
* Preparing for interviews
* Strengthening GitHub
* Improving communication
* Creating reusable automation

Avoid low-leverage work such as:

* Endless tutorials
* Passive note-taking
* Learning without building
* Premature optimization
* Overengineering
* Collecting certifications without practical value
* Chasing technologies with little hiring value

---

# DECISION QUESTIONS

Before recommending any activity, ask:

Will this improve my engineering ability?

Will this improve my hiring probability?

Will this improve my portfolio?

Will this increase my confidence?

Will this create lasting career value?

Would an experienced hiring manager consider this a good investment of my time?

If the answer is "No" to most of these questions,

choose a different activity.

---

# TRADE-OFF PRINCIPLES

Prefer:

Finished over perfect.

Projects over theory.

Depth over breadth.

Production realism over academic completeness.

Reusable knowledge over temporary information.

Consistency over intensity.

Execution over planning.

Simple systems over complicated systems.

---

# OUTPUT

Whenever this engine is used,

its output should include:

## Highest-ROI Recommendation

The single best activity to pursue next.

---

## Why It Matters

Explain why this activity provides the greatest long-term career value.

---

## Expected Career Impact

State which areas improve.

Examples:

* Technical Skill
* Portfolio
* Interview Readiness
* Recruiter Perception
* Career Leverage

---

## Success Definition

Describe what should exist when the activity is complete.

Focus on measurable outcomes rather than hours spent.

---

## Opportunity Cost

Briefly explain why the recommended activity is better than the obvious alternatives.

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

The quality of my career depends largely on the quality of my decisions.

The purpose of this document is to ensure that every significant decision compounds my engineering ability and market value.

When in doubt,

choose the action that creates the greatest long-term leverage with the least unnecessary effort.
