# DEVOPS CAREER EXECUTION ROADMAP

Version: 2.0

Purpose:

This document defines the long-term execution strategy of my Career Operating System.

It answers one question:

**"What capabilities, proof, and career outcomes must exist before I move to the next stage?"**

This document is strategic.

It should change only when my long-term career direction changes.

---

# CAREER NORTH STAR

Become a highly employable DevOps Engineer who:

* Builds production-quality systems confidently.
* Demonstrates practical engineering ability through real projects.
* Performs consistently well in technical interviews.
* Is competitive for strong product companies.
* Continuously increases market value throughout my career.

Every significant activity should improve one or more of:

* Technical Skill
* Proof of Skill
* Practical Confidence
* Communication
* Interview Readiness
* Recruiter Perception
* Career Leverage
* Market Value

# DECISION FILTER

Before investing significant time in any activity, ask:

Does this improve:

* Hiring Probability?
* Market Value?
* Proof of Skill?
* Practical Confidence?
* Interview Performance?
* Recruiter Perception?
* Career Leverage?

If the answer is "No" to most of these, reconsider whether the activity deserves your time.

# ROADMAP EVOLUTION

This roadmap is intentionally stable.

It should **not** change after normal learning sessions.

Routine work belongs in the Active Workspace.

Review this roadmap only when:

* My long-term career direction changes.
* I complete a major career milestone.
* I successfully transition into a new DevOps role.
* An Evolution Session identifies a strategic improvement.

The roadmap should evolve slowly.

Execution should happen inside the Active Workspace.
