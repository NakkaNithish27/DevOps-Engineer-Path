# CAREER SYSTEM REVIEW

Version: 2.0

Purpose:

This document governs the evolution of my Career Operating System.

Its purpose is not to review my career.

Its purpose is to review whether my **Career Operating System itself** remains effective.

Like any well-designed engineering system, this Career Operating System will gradually drift away from its original design.

This review exists to detect that drift, remove unnecessary complexity, and ensure the system continues supporting my career rather than becoming another project.

---

# REVIEW TRIGGERS

Perform a Career System Review only when meaningful change has occurred.

Examples include:

* Completing a major project.
* Completing a significant career milestone.
* Transitioning into a new role.
* Receiving repeated interview feedback.
* Feeling that progress has stalled.
* Feeling that maintaining the Career Operating System has become burdensome.
* Discovering a significantly better workflow.
* Approximately every 6–12 months if no other trigger occurs.

Time alone should not trigger a review.

Meaningful change should.

---

# REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

JARVIS should request only the documents required for the review.

Normally these include:

* Career Operating System
* Career Execution Roadmap
* Active Workspace
* Career Pipeline
* Engineering Knowledge Base

Additional documents should only be included if they are directly relevant.

---

# REVIEW OBJECTIVES

The review should answer five questions.

## 1. Career Alignment

Am I still moving toward my Career North Star?

Are my long-term goals still correct?

Has the job market changed?

Has my definition of success changed?

---

## 2. Execution Quality

Does my day-to-day execution match my strategy?

Am I consistently working on high-leverage activities?

Am I spending enough time building?

What repeatedly creates the greatest career value?

What repeatedly wastes time?

---

## 3. Career Feedback

What is the market telling me?

Examples:

Recruiter interest

Interview feedback

Project quality

Portfolio effectiveness

Resume effectiveness

Communication effectiveness

Where is the current hiring bottleneck?

---

## 4. Engineering Knowledge

Is my Engineering Knowledge Base becoming genuinely more valuable?

Are important patterns being captured?

Is unnecessary information accumulating?

What knowledge gaps are limiting my growth?

---

## 5. System Health

This is the most important section.

Review the Career Operating System itself.

Questions:

Does the system still reduce decision fatigue?

Does the system still encourage building?

Am I spending more time engineering than maintaining the system?

Is any document becoming unnecessarily large?

Are responsibilities between documents still clear?

Has unnecessary complexity appeared?

Could anything be removed?

Could anything be simplified?

The default answer should always favor simplicity.

---

# EVOLUTION DECISIONS

At the end of every review determine:

Should the Career Operating System change?

Should the Career Roadmap change?

Should the Active Workspace structure change?

Should the Career Pipeline change?

Should the Engineering Knowledge Base change?

Should the Career Decision Engine change?

Should JARVIS change?

Every proposed change should answer:

"What problem does this solve?"

If no meaningful problem exists,

do not change the system.

---

# REVIEW OUTPUT

The review should produce:

## What Should Stay

Identify the parts of the Career Operating System that are working well.

Avoid changing successful components without a compelling reason.

---

## What Should Improve

Identify only the highest-impact improvements.

Avoid cosmetic changes.

---

## What Should Be Removed

Identify unnecessary complexity.

Prefer deleting over adding.

If a document, workflow, or process no longer creates meaningful career value,

remove it.

---

## Action Plan

Produce a short action plan.

Prioritize:

1. Highest-impact improvements.
2. Simplifications.
3. Structural changes only if they significantly improve the system.

---

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

During every review, remember:

The Career Operating System exists to support engineering growth.

It should never become another engineering project.

Every document should have exactly one responsibility.

Every workflow should minimize decision fatigue.

Every improvement should reduce maintenance while increasing career value.

When in doubt,

choose the simpler system.

---

# SUCCESS CRITERIA

This review succeeds when:

* The Career Operating System becomes simpler over time.
* Maintenance effort decreases instead of increasing.
* My engineering output continues increasing.
* My career growth accelerates.
* The system remains almost invisible during normal work.

The best Career Operating System is not the most sophisticated one.

It is the one that quietly helps me become a better engineer while demanding almost nothing in return.
