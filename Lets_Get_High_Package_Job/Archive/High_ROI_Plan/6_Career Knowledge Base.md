# ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE BASE

Version: 2.0

Purpose:

This document is the permanent engineering memory of my Career Operating System.

It is not a notebook.

It is not a collection of study notes.

It is not documentation copied from the internet.

It is my personal engineering handbook.

Everything stored here should continue creating value years from now.

The objective is not to remember everything.

The objective is to remember what repeatedly makes me a better engineer.

This document should only be updated during Evolution Sessions.

---

# KNOWLEDGE PRINCIPLES

Before adding anything, ask:

* Will Future Me likely reuse this?
* Did this solve a real engineering problem?
* Does this improve how I think?
* Is this technology-independent or broadly reusable?
* Would I want this available during an interview?
* Would I be disappointed if I forgot this?

If the answer is "No" to most of these,

do not store it here.

Keep it in the Active Workspace instead.

Quality is more important than quantity.
